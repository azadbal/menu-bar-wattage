# Statusbar Power Privacy Statement

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Statusbar Power is designed to work entirely on your Mac.

## Data collection

Statusbar Power does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or subscriptions.

## Network access

The app does not make network requests. It reads power-source information provided locally by macOS’s public power-source APIs and displays the result in the menu bar.

## Information shown in the app

The app may read local power-source state and the charging adapter wattage reported by macOS. This information is used only to calculate and display the current menu-bar value while the app is running. Statusbar Power does not upload or retain that information.

## Support requests

If you voluntarily open a GitHub issue, GitHub processes the information you choose to include under [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement). The app does not automatically send diagnostics or create support requests.

## Changes

If this statement changes, the updated version will be published in this repository with a new effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/azadbal/statusbar_power/issues/new) and label it as a privacy question.
